I build and roll out AI coding agents that teams can trust: specs, gates and evals. Maintainer of sdd-skills.

## What I do now

I'm a Senior Engineering Lead at Toyota Connected Europe, where I also lead development standards. I run a programme that sets engineering standards across several vendor organisations, and I work on how teams adopt AI coding tools.

## What I build in the open

- [sdd-skills](https://github.com/georgeracu/sdd-skills): agent skills for spec-driven development. A review gate blocks the next step on a critical finding, and evals measure whether the skills help.
- [google-jules-mcp-server](https://github.com/georgeracu/google-jules-mcp-server): an MCP server that lets Claude and other MCP clients drive Google's Jules coding agent. It's on npm and in the MCP registry.
- [testcontainers-moto](https://github.com/georgeracu/testcontainers-moto): a Testcontainers module for Moto, so JVM tests can use AWS services in one local container. It's on Maven Central.

## Where I came from

Before Toyota I was a lead consultant at Thoughtworks, from 2019 to 2021. Before that I worked mostly on payments and e-commerce, at the Government Digital Service, Transport for London and Master of Malt.
